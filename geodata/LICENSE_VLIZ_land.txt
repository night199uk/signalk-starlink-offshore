ATTRIBUTION & LICENSE - World Countries 2014 land
=================================================

Source data:
  Marine Regions (VLIZ) "World Countries 2014"
  The normal baseline coastline of the Maritime Boundaries Geodatabase, from
  which the Territorial Seas (12NM) and Internal Waters layers were calculated.
  https://www.marineregions.org/
  Accessed via the VLIZ WFS layer `MarineRegions:worldcountries_esri_2014`
  (https://geo.vliz.be/geoserver) by `tools/fetch-vliz-land.js`.
